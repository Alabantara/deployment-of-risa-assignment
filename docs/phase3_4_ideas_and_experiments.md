# CampusFest Kolkata (Challenge B) — Phase 3 & Phase 4: Growth Bets, Prioritization & Experimentation

## Phase 3: Candidate Pool, RICE Prioritization & Overlap Haircuts

### 3.1 Methodology & Constraint Guardrails
All 10 candidate strategies were evaluated against the Phase 2 quantitative model (**Base: `₹17,23,800`**, **Stretch: `₹30,98,542`**, **Incremental Stretch Gap: `₹13,74,742`**) and three hard resource constraints:
* **Budget Cap:** `₹1,00,000` total acquisition & activation budget (`₹25,000` incremental over Base `₹75,000`).
* **Team Bandwidth Cap:** `480` hours (`4` owners × `10 hrs/wk` × `12 weeks`).
* **Inventory Cap:** `32` cash sponsorship slots (`1` Title, `3` Associate, `8` Category, `10` Event, `10` Booth).

**RICE Formula:** $\text{RICE} = \frac{\text{Reach (1–10)} \times \text{Impact (1–10)} \times \text{Confidence (\%)}}{\text{Effort (1–10)}}$
* *Reach* measures share of final deal funnel touched; *Impact* maps directly to Phase 2 Gross Bridge Cash (`10` ≥ ₹4.0L, `8–9` = ₹3.0–3.9L, `5–6` = ₹1.7–2.9L); *Confidence* reflects Phase 1 dataset evidence strength.
* **Overlap Haircut (`5%–10%`):** Applied to each selected bet so combined contributions do **not** double-count improvements across funnel stages and strictly stay **below** the `₹13,74,742` Stretch ceiling.

### 3.3 Detailed Execution Cards for the 5 Selected Bets

#### 1. `I1` — Early-Bird Milestone & Prime-Slot Lock-In ("Cash-First Tranches")
* **Concept & Why It Works:** Directly attacks Challenge B's core "signed vs. cash collected" gap. Standard campus contracts hold `20–30%` post-event PoP, and `Cobalt Learning` (`₹80k`) withdrew `23` days post-signing (`4.0%` slippage). Restructuring contracts to **50% on MoU signing + 50% by T-7 days (`Apr 5`)**—rewarded with first-pick main-gate/stage stall locations, GST-ready instant invoicing, and a `10%` bonus creator reel package—lifts pre-fest collection from `78% → 90%`.
* **Net Incremental ₹:** **`₹3,72,000`** (`10%` haircut on `₹4,13,139`) | **Budget:** `₹12,000` (Razorpay/gateway fees, fast GST/MoU templates, priority signage) | **Owner:** `Meera Iyer` | **Timeline:** `Weeks 3–12`.
* **Test Design:** Pilot Early-Bird terms on the first `10` proposals reaching `negotiating` in Weeks 3–5 vs. historical `30/50/20` baseline; measure acceptance and days-to-advance before locking as default.
* **Primary KPI:** Pre-Fest Cash Collection Rate (`≥ 90%`), Median Days Signing → 50% Advance (`≤ 5 days`), Post-Signing Withdrawal Rate (`0%`).

#### 2. `I2` — Zero-Drop Inbound & Referral CRM Fast-Track ("24-Hr Opp Gate")
* **Concept & Why It Works:** Fixes the single highest-confidence leak in Phase 1: **`18` of `60` qualified brands (`30%`) never got an opportunity**, including `6` from `SC04` (Partner Interest Form) and `6` from `SC05` (Creator Referrals) — both **`100%` historical win-rate channels**. Enforcing a **24-hr SLA** via automated CRM webhook + pre-built vertical proposal templates lifts `Conv → Proposal` from `80.0% → 87.5%` while hard-blocking unqualified opps (`15` premature opps eliminated).
* **Net Incremental ₹:** **`₹1,70,000`** (`5%` haircut on `₹1,79,010`) | **Budget:** `₹8,000` (CRM/Supabase webhook automation & template design) | **Owner:** `Kabir Shah` | **Timeline:** `Weeks 1–12`.
* **Test Design:** Week 1–4 audit comparing `SC04`/`SC05` qualified leads processed under the `24h` auto-opp SLA vs. historical `5.12`-day median delay.
* **Primary KPI:** `% Qualified Leads Actioned ≤ 24h` (`≥ 90%`), `Conversation → Proposal Rate` (`≥ 87.5%`), Non-Qualified Opp Leakage (`0%`).

#### 3. `I3` — Category-Exclusivity Tiering & Kolkata Flagship Title Bundle
* **Concept & Why It Works:** Historical cash deals capped at `₹2.00L` (`Beardo`) with `0` Title Sponsors, averaging `₹1,00,000`, despite St. Xavier's Kolkata Associate tier pricing at `₹3.00L`. Creating **8 Category-Exclusive Cash Slots (`₹1,35,000`, +25% rival lock-out premium)** across F&B, BFSI, EV/Mobility, EdTech, Personal Care, Apparel, Tech, and Gaming—plus **1 Flagship Title Slot (`₹4,00,000`)** targeted at Kolkata HQs (ITC, Emami, Bandhan Bank, Exide, Sector V tech)—lifts average cash deal size `+15%` to `₹1,15,000`.
* **Net Incremental ₹:** **`₹3,22,000`** (`8%` haircut on `₹3,50,270`) | **Budget:** `₹18,000` (bespoke Kolkata HQ pitch decks, physical sample kits, competitor-lockout collateral) | **Owner:** `Riya Kapoor` | **Timeline:** `Weeks 1–10`.
* **Test Design:** Pitch exclusivity tiering to the first `15` senior HQ meetings in Weeks 1–5; track shortlist preference vs. standard `₹1.25L` rate card.
* **Primary KPI:** Average Cash Deal Size (`≥ ₹1,15,000`), Category-Exclusive Slot Fill (`≥ 6/8` by Week 8), Title/Associate Deals Closed (`≥ 4`).

#### 4. `I4` — ICP Sponsor-Scoring & Anti-Bloat Filter ("Stop Cold Media Bleed")
* **Concept & Why It Works:** Eliminates two destructive dataset drags: (a) **`6` of `7` losses (`85.7%`) were cold `Media` pitched at `₹1.25L` cash (`0%` win rate)**, and (b) **`100%` of wins came via `Partnerships Manager` contacts** (`0` via `Brand Marketing Lead`). A **100-point ICP Fit Score** (`+30` Partnerships Manager verified, `+30` high-win vertical F&B/Mobility/FMCG/BFSI/EdTech, `+20` Kolkata HQ/regional office, `+20` youth spend history) gates outbound (`Score ≥ 60`), routes `Media` to `₹50k–₹75k` in-kind/barter, and enforces a **Day-7 stalled-proposal pivot/archive** (since won deals close in `5d` post-proposal vs. `11d` for losses).
* **Net Incremental ₹:** **`₹2,22,000`** (`10%` haircut on `₹2,46,683`) | **Budget:** `₹20,000` (LinkedIn/Apollo enrichment for `Partnerships Manager` verification) | **Owner:** `Kabir Shah` | **Timeline:** `Weeks 1–12`.
* **Test Design:** Score `60` outbound Kolkata prospects in Weeks 1–4; monitor meeting rate, `Media` barter conversion, and `>7d` stall drop-off.
* **Primary KPI:** `Proposal → Net Signed Win Rate` (`≥ 54.0%`), `Cash Deal Mix` (`≥ 88.0%`), Cold `Media` `₹1.25L` Losses (`0`), Stalled Proposals `>7d` (`≤ 10%`).

#### 5. `I5` — Kolkata Alumni & Creator-Bundled Warm Intro Engine
* **Concept & Why It Works:** `SC02` (Warm Intros) & `SC05` (Creator Referrals) generated **`65%` of all historical cash (`₹13.65L`)** from just `30` attempts (`94.4%` opp win rate), while `SC01` Cold Email burned `224` attempts for `0` wins. Reallocating cold email hours into (a) **Kolkata Alumni Warm Mapping** into ITC, Emami, Bandhan Bank, PwC, TCS, and Cognizant (`SC02`), and (b) **Micro-Creator Co-Pitch Bundles** pairing campus creators + QR lead-capture booths (`SC05`) lifts `Prospect → Conversation` from `32.5% → 36.0%`.
* **Net Incremental ₹:** **`₹1,67,000`** (`10%` haircut on `₹1,85,640`) | **Budget:** `₹42,000` (`₹28k` campus micro-creator reel retainers + `₹14k` alumni showcase) | **Owner:** `Nisha Rao` | **Timeline:** `Weeks 1–11`.
* **Test Design:** Launch `20` warm alumni/creator-bundled pitches in Weeks 1–4 (`10` F&B/FMCG, `10` Mobility/BFSI) and benchmark meeting conversion against `17%` historical cold rate.
* **Primary KPI:** `Prospect → Meeting Rate` (`≥ 36.0%` blended, `≥ 65%` warm), Warm/Referral Share of Meetings (`≥ 65%`).

### 3.4 Explicitly Rejected / Deprioritized Ideas (and Why)
* **`R1` — Paid LinkedIn InMail Ads (`SC03` Boost, RICE `2.00`):** `SC03` had a `0/12` close rate in the dataset (`2` lost, `1` withdrawn, `9` stalled); ₹35k ad spend yields far lower ROI than `₹42k` on `SC05` Creator Bundles (`100%` historical opp win rate).
* **`R2` — External Commission-Based Brokers (`RICE 1.96`):** `15–25%` agency fees erode `₹3L–₹5L+` cash margin, push non-ICP barter leads, and undermine `T-7d` upfront cash collection discipline.
* **`R3` — Micro-Merchandise & Coupon Booklets (`₹5k–₹15k`, `RICE 0.89`):** High servicing time (`2–3 hrs/deal`) breaches the `480-hour` team cap while generating negligible cash and cannibalizing `₹55k` Booth slots.
* **`R4` — Celebrity Pro-Night Co-Production Split (`RICE 0.56`):** Requires `₹3L–₹5L+` upfront artist guarantee (`3–5x` over our entire `₹1,00,000` budget cap) with severe cash-flow downside.
* **`R5` — High-Volume Cold Email Expansion (`SC01 → 500+ Brands`, `RICE 0.40`):** Dataset proved `SC01` burned `224` attempts (`60.5%` of effort) for **`0` wins** and caused all `7` opt-outs. Scaling it wastes team hours and burns Kolkata HQ goodwill.
